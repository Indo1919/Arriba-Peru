# Arriba Perú

A co-designed mobile platform bringing safety, clarity and opportunity to Peru’s domestic workers.
Bilingual (English / Español) hi-fi prototype by **Matias Indacochea** — Syracuse University, Industrial & Interaction Design capstone, 2026.

## What’s here

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Product page: live app in the hero, a one-minute film built from the real screens, and the feature tour |
| `prototype.html` | The app on its own. On a phone it runs full screen like a native app |

Both files are fully self-contained (no build step). Open either one in a browser, or turn on **GitHub Pages** (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root) to get a live link.

## The app

Android-first, built on Material 3 expressive patterns, Google Sans Flex, and a tonal palette from the brand burgundy.

- **Jobs** — verified families, anonymous family ratings, and a pay check against Peru’s legal minimum wage for the exact hours offered
- **Learn** — rights from Ley N.° 31047 in six color-coded categories, every screen read aloud
- **Wallet** — Yape and cash in one place, with late or partial payments flagged
- **Videos** — step-by-step lessons with a listen-only mode
- **Safety** — discreet check-ins, quick exit, one-tap incident reporting, and a slide-to-confirm emergency alert

Co-designed with 15 domestic workers in Lima, with guidance from medical anthropologist Dr. María Amalia Pesantes.

## Notes

Demo families, names and amounts are fictional. Legal content follows Ley N.° 31047 and its regulation, with the minimum wage in effect since October 1, 2026 (S/ 1,230).
